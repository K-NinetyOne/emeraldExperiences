About us

We are a husband (Barry) and wife (Pauline) team from Ireland.

Having spent years travelling and seeing the world and all of the amazing things it has to offer we decided that we want to share our own country with those who want to see it.

We learned so much about the world on our travels but also what adventurous types want and need from their trips abroad!

Come and join us on some of our curated experiences to see the real side of Ireland that you may not get to see on the main stream travel websites!

We are based in Carlow, most of the experiences on offer are directly available from us but we also partner with other companies for deals, packages and discounts!

All of these can be found on our Experiences or our Booking Page


- Barry and Pauline
